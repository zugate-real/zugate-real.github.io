Plans and payments

How do I cancel Yearly?

Yearly renews each year until you cancel it, and Apple runs it.

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then Three.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Do it at least a day before it renews. The year you paid for runs to its end.

Cancelled or not, everything you did stays on your phone, and your first habit stays free.

Apple's guide to cancelling
I paid on another phone. How do I get it back?

In Three, open Settings → Purchase → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you paid with. There's no Three account, so nothing else is needed. Lifetime and Yearly both come back this way.

Your habits and days move separately. See moving to a new phone.

Why can't I mark anything?

Your first habit is free, for good. A second or a third needs a plan: Yearly, or Lifetime, which is one payment. The App Store shows the price for your country.

If a plan ends with more than one habit going, marking waits from the day it ended. Choose a plan again, or pause the others and keep one going. Either way, the days in between aren't counted as missed.

Your record stays either way. You can still look back through it and export it.

If you've already paid, try Restore purchases.

Can I get a refund?

Apple takes the payment, so Apple handles refunds too. Three never sees your payment.

Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the purchase. If Apple says no and you think that's wrong, write to me and I'll say what I can do.

Your record

How do I move to a new phone?

On the old phone, open Settings → Move to another phone. Three makes one locked file and shows a code.

  1. Send the file to the new phone, with AirDrop, Messages or Files.
  2. Open it in Three on the new phone.
  3. Type the code shown on the old phone.

It brings every habit and every day, with their notes, moods and photos. The file is locked with the code, so send the file and keep the code to yourself. Then restore your purchase on the new phone.

If you set up the new phone from a backup of the old one, your days come along with it.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Settings → Export your days writes one file with your habits, your days and your notes. Save it to Files or send it wherever you like. It's yours. Photos stay in the app. To take them along, use Move to another phone.

Nothing is kept anywhere but your phone, so if you delete the app, your record goes with it. Export first.

How do I erase everything?

Settings → Start over erases every habit, every day, and every note, photo and mood on this phone. There's no undo, so export first if you want to keep anything. Earlier backups of your phone keep their copy until they're replaced.

What stays is what isn't your record: your settings, the random number shared records are filed under, and the date Three first opened. The privacy page explains each.

Reminders and widgets

My reminders aren't arriving
  • Three asks about reminders after your first sealed day, never on first launch. If you said no then, turn them on in Three's Settings → Reminders.
  • Check that iOS allows them: Settings on your iPhone, then Notifications → Three. If they're off, Three's own Settings says so.
  • A Focus, such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb, can hold them back.
  • A habit you've already done that day isn't reminded about. That's on purpose.
  • Reminders move toward when you usually do a habit, so the time can shift a little. Each habit's own time is on its page.
The widget doesn't update, or shows yesterday

Open Three once. It refreshes every widget. If a Lock Screen widget still shows the old day, remove it and add it again.

How do I mark a habit without opening Three?

Add a widget and tap a habit on it.

  • Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot until the apps jiggle, tap Edit, then Add Widget (on iOS 17, the + at the top), and choose Three.
  • Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise, choose the Lock Screen, tap the row under the clock and choose Three.

You can also tap Done on a reminder, say "Mark a habit done in Three" to Siri, or use Three on Apple Watch.

Privacy

What leaves my phone?

Nothing you write. Your habits, days, notes, photos and moods stay on the phone.

If you'd like to help make Three better, you can turn on Settings → Share how you use Three. Short records of how the app is used, like which screens you open and what you tap, then go to PostHog, are stored in the EU, and are deleted after a year. They never include a habit's name, anything you wrote or the time you did something. It starts off.

What's shared, and why
Can you delete my usage records?

Honestly, not one person's, today. The records carry no name, and the app doesn't show you the random number they're filed under, so neither of us can pick out yours.

What works: turn off Settings → Share how you use Three. That stops new ones at once and deletes any still waiting on your phone. What was already sent is deleted a year after it arrived.

About Three

Why only three habits?

Because three is what people keep. When one becomes automatic, it graduates and leaves, and that makes room. If you truly need a fourth, Three asks once and lets you.

What does it mean when a habit graduates?

Now and then, Three asks whether a habit feels automatic. Say yes twice in a row and it moves to your shelf, which frees its place. It's yours now. If it slips, you can bring it back.
