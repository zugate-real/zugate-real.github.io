Three · Support

One person made this. Write to him.

Email three.support.indie@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and which iOS version, and what you expected to happen. Replies come from the developer, usually within a day or two.

The widget doesn't update

Open the app once; it refreshes every widget. If a Lock Screen widget still shows the old day, remove it and add it again from the Lock Screen's Customise screen.

I bought Lifetime on my old phone

Open the app, go past the paywall to Restore Purchases, and sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Nothing else is needed, because there is no Three account.

I didn't get a reminder

Reminders are asked for after your first sealed day, never at launch. Check Settings → Notifications → Three on your phone, and Reminders inside the app. A habit you did before its reminder time will not be reminded about that day; that is on purpose.

I want my data

Settings → Export your days writes one file with every habit and every day. It's yours. Nothing is kept anywhere but your phone, so if you delete the app, the record goes with it; export first.

I want a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too — Three never sees your payment. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account, and choose the purchase. If Apple turns it down and you think that is wrong, write to us and we will say what we can.

I want my data deleted

Settings → Start over removes every habit, every day, every note, photo and mood from this phone, and there is no copy anywhere else. Share how you use Three starts off. If you turned it on and want the usage records deleted too, email us: they carry no name or account, only an id drawn at random on your phone, so tell us roughly which days you used the app and we will have them removed. Turning the switch off stops any more being sent and clears anything still waiting on your phone.

Why only three habits?

Because three is what people keep. When one becomes automatic it graduates and makes room. If you truly need a fourth, the app will ask you once and let you.
