# About

I'm Kumar Shubham, a senior iOS engineer in Bengaluru. I work at the platform
layer of a 20+ module Swift codebase at Allen Digital — the kind of work that
sits under every feature team: decoding infrastructure, an ActivityKit
framework, networking primitives, and multi-release platform migrations.
Before that I shipped payments and booking flows at redBus, India's largest
bus-ticketing platform.

I'm the author of [MDLatex](https://github.com/Allen-Digital-OSS/iOSMDLatex-oss),
an open-source Swift package for rendering Markdown with embedded LaTeX, used
in production to power AI physics and math tutoring.

This blog is mostly about the sharp edges of the iOS platform — the behavior
that isn't in the documentation and that you only find at scale.
